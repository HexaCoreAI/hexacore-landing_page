Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to do AI Data Annotation?
No. You simply provide the verified account. Our vetted, highly trained workers complete the complex RLHF, prompt engineering, and reasoning tasks.
Is it safe for my account?
Yes. Our Technical Infrastructure team uses strict operational protocols, localized network routing, and secure browsers to mimic natural, single-user activity and prevent platform bans.
When and how do I get paid?
We process payouts weekly. Once the platform releases funds to our secure corporate accounts, our Finance Lead disburses your 30% share directly to your bank account at the prevailing premium exchange rate.
What happens if an account gets suspended?
While our security protocols minimize this risk, platform suspensions can happen. If it does, our team handles the appeals. You will never be charged or penalized for operational losses.
